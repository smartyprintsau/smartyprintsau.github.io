About us

A small Queensland maker with a big heart for kids

Smarty Prints is a little home-based business with a 3D printer, a Cricut and a lot of ideas. We've spent years around children, home-schooling and people with disabilities, and we kept wishing for routine boards and learning tools that were sturdy, personal and actually cute.

So we started making them. Every picture on our visual tiles and garden labels is drawn by us, and every piece is printed to order for your family, classroom or garden.

What we make

  • Visual supports: First/Then, choice, routine, weekly and token boards
  • Home-school & learning: maths, literacy and game pieces
  • Classroom & school: name plates, tags, labels and stamps
  • Garden: plant stakes and hang tags with 700+ pictures
  • Gifts: teacher gifts, baby keepsakes and keyrings
  • Printables: PDF picture cards to print at home

Questions

Good to know

Can't see your question? Email us and we'll get back to you.

How much is postage?

Postage depends on the size of your order. We'll tell you the exact cost with your proof, before you pay.

How long will my order take?

Everything is made to order. We'll give you a time frame with your proof, and let you know when your order has been posted.

Can I change the pictures, words or colours?

Yes, that's the whole idea! Add your notes to each item in your list, and we'll show you a proof before printing.

Are the prices on the website final?

They're a guide. Some items start from the price shown, and your proof will show the final price. Prices are in Australian dollars. We're not registered for GST, so no GST is included. Minimum order $15.

Are these toys safe for little ones?

Many of our pieces (tiles, tokens, counters and puzzle pieces) are small parts. They're not suitable for children under 3 and should be used with adult supervision. Products with magnets have them sealed inside, but always keep magnets away from young children.

Can I buy the printables for my classroom?

Yes. Printable PDFs are for personal and classroom use. Please don't resell or share the files.
